Bubbles for G2

iMessage on your glasses, through your own Mac.

First time? Set up your Mac with the guide at github.com/whtrbt7/bubbles-for-g2. On the Mac, run:
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/whtrbt7/bubbles-for-g2/main/get.sh | bash
then paste the URL and token it prints below.
Using Claude Code? Tell it: "Install Bubbles for G2 by following AGENT_INSTALL.md from github.com/whtrbt7/bubbles-for-g2".

Glasses: swipe to move, tap to open, double-tap to go back.
In a thread: tap to reply, hold to dictate. Sends wait 5 s; double-tap cancels.
Your phone must be connected to Tailscale.
