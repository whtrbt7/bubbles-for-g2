Read and send your iMessages from your G2 glasses, through your own Mac (BlueBubbles + the Bubbles gateway over Tailscale). Setup guide: SETUP.md in the download.

ON THE GLASSES
- Inbox: newest conversations first, unread marked with *. Scroll to "More..." for older chats.
- Tap a chat to read it. Swipe up for older messages.
- Favorites: tap the "> Favorites" row, or press and hold on the inbox.
- Reply: tap in a chat for quick replies, or hold to dictate and release when done.
- New message: "+ New message", then pick a favorite or say a name.
- Sending waits 5 seconds. Double-tap to cancel.
- New texts pop up while the app is open: tap to open, hold to reply, double-tap to dismiss.
- Double-tap goes back. Double-tap on the first inbox page exits.

WITH EVEN AI ("Hey Even")
Set Even AI > Agent Configuration to your gateway's /v1/chat/completions URL and agent token. Then say:
- "Read my messages" or "Any new texts" - your latest texts from the last day.
- "What did Sam say" - your last 3 messages with that person.
- "Text Sam I'm on my way" (also "message", "tell", "send a text to") - asks you to confirm first.
- "Reply on my way" - replies to whoever texted you last, after you confirm.
- "Send" (or "yes", "go ahead") - sends the waiting message.
- "Cancel" (or "no") - drops it. Unconfirmed messages expire after 2 minutes.
Nothing is ever sent without your spoken confirmation. Favorites can be called by first name.
Other questions get this command list; switch Agent Configuration back to Even AI for general questions.

Requires: a Mac running Messages and BlueBubbles, the Bubbles gateway, and Tailscale on your phone.
