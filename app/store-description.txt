Read and send iMessage and SMS from your G2 glasses, through your own Mac. No cloud service: messages travel only between your iPhone, your Mac and your glasses.

SETUP (once, about 20 minutes): github.com/whtrbt7/bubbles-for-g2
1. On your Mac: sign in to Messages, install BlueBubbles Server and Tailscale (the guide walks you through it).
2. In Terminal: curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/whtrbt7/bubbles-for-g2/main/get.sh | bash
3. Open Bubbles for G2 on your phone and paste the URL and token the installer prints.
Using Claude Code? Tell it to follow AGENT_INSTALL.md from the GitHub page.
Not set up yet? Tap the glasses on the first screen to try a demo with made-up conversations.

ON THE GLASSES
- Inbox: newest first, unread marked *, "More..." for older chats.
- Tap a chat to read it; swipe up for older messages.
- Favorites: "> Favorites" row, or press and hold on the inbox. Pick and reorder them on the phone page.
- Reply: tap for quick replies, or hold to dictate (speech-to-text runs on your Mac).
- Every send waits 5 seconds. Double-tap to cancel.
- New texts pop up while the app is open: tap to open, hold to reply, double-tap to dismiss.

"HEY EVEN" (optional, Even Hub developer accounts)
"Read my messages" - "What did Jordan say" - "Text Jordan I'm on my way", then "send" or "cancel".
